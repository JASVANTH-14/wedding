OUR LOVE STORY

Some love stories begin with fireworks. Theirs began with a quiet glance across a crowded room — the kind of moment you almost miss, but never forget.

He noticed the way she laughed, unguarded and warm. She noticed the way he listened — truly listened — as though every word she spoke mattered.

What started as stolen conversations turned into long evening walks, where the world faded and only their words remained.

They discovered they shared the same silences — the comfortable kind, where being together was enough.

Through seasons of change they held on, learning that love isn't just a feeling but a choice you make every single day.

He learned her strength. She discovered his tenderness. Together, they became more than they ever were apart.

And then one ordinary evening, under a sky painted in gold, he asked her a question that changed everything.

She said yes — not because it was perfect, but because with him, even the imperfect moments felt like home.

Now they stand at the beginning of forever, ready to write every chapter together — with love, with laughter, and with faith.
